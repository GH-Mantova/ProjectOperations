Changed from the first pass
Six CRM items became three. No collapsible parents — each item opens a page whose tabs carry what would otherwise have been nav children.
Accounts · List, Relationships
Relationships stops being its own nav item and becomes the second tab — it is account data either way.
Tenders · Register, Follow-ups
One list, two saved views. Follow-ups is the register filtered to what is overdue or has no next action. Badge = overdue count.
Comms hub · Inbox, Threads, To-dos
Lead intake becomes the Inbox tab. Untriaged leads arrive here; once triaged they leave. Badge = untriaged + overdue to-dos.
One caveat on the merge
The Inbox is intake’s screen sitting in the comms window. In code it stays the intake module — comms deliberately imports nothing from Tender or Job so it can branch off later. Same window for you, boundary intact underneath.
