Next action is an output, not a field you type. Pressing Log records what happened — channel, who, what was said — and sets the next action in the same step. That is why Last interaction, Logged by and Next action sit together: the first two produce the third. A row whose next action is “None set” is one nobody has logged against, which is exactly what the Follow-ups tab filters for.
