Where this breaks from the old prompt — for you to confirm

It's "planned", not "spend". The log records tip recommendations someone accepted — the plan and its price on the day — not dockets or invoices. So the tab says Planned tipping cost. Calling it spend would put a planning figure where people expect actuals.
Tender-time plans need to follow the job. Today the log keeps projectId but drops the tender when a pick is made at tender stage (tip-recommendations.service.ts:318-327), so every plan made while pricing the job would be missing from this tab. The rewrite adds tenderId to the log (nullable) and the tab shows rows from the job and the tender it came from — the Tender chip above.
It needs a migration. Tips have no "prices reviewed" date today — MapLocation has no such field. The rewrite adds pricesReviewedAt (nullable, so every existing tip starts as Never reviewed). The old prompt's "No migration" is wrong.
