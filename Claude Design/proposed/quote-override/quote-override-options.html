Overriding a line on a client quote: what should happen?

The same quote in every card: Line A Demolition $80,000 (you override it to $75,000), Line B Waste removal $20,000, adjustment +10%. Each card shows the editor inputs, then what the client sees on the PDF.

The +10% is worked out on the original $100,000, so the total ignores your override. Line B then soaks up the whole $10,000 adjustment.

Use it when: you want that line to read a set figure, and the markup should only apply to everything else. Watch: the total drops when you override down, so your margin moves.

Whichever you pick, the fix also adds a check: the rows must add up to the printed total, and the quote won't render if they don't. That stops this class of bug coming back.
