Density and colour are two separate choices and combine freely. Colour follows your 25 Sep ruling: light mode only. Printed and PDF documents are not affected by either choice.

Two calls before the spec

1. Where your choice is remembered. Today both settings live only in the browser, and the colour choice is wiped every time you log out. As built, a personal colour scheme would last until the next sign-out.
  1. Save it to your account (recommended). It follows you to any computer or tablet. It is applied when you sign in and removed from the browser when you sign out, so a shared site tablet never shows the last person's choice. Needs one additive field on the user record (a migration, so that build PR stops for you) and a small "my preferences" route.
  2. Keep it in the browser, and stop wiping it at logout. No migration. But it is per device: set it at the office and the site tablet won't know. On a shared tablet the next person inherits it.
2. What a person may pick.
  1. Only the company's saved schemes (recommended, shown above). They are admin-approved and contrast-checked. Needs a read-only list of schemes that any signed-in user can see; today only admins can list them.
  2. Their own colours. Not recommended: it breaks a consistent brand on screen and skips the contrast check the builder enforces.
