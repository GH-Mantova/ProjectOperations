Haulage cycle and load capacity

The screens behind S8 and S9, and what they produce. Every figure is an example, not your data. Two new ideas appear on a waste line: how long the run to the tip takes, and how much goes on the truck. Both fill themselves in and both can be overruled by whoever is pricing.

What this changes, for you to confirm

Nothing existing moves. No saved line is repriced or resized. The filled-in figures only appear where the field was left empty.
A waste line now points at a tip. Today it holds the tip's name as text, which nothing can route to. The line gets a real link to the map location, and without one there is no cycle and no route.
Straight line is never chosen, only fallen back to. It is badged "estimated, no route" and the estimator cannot select it. Once Azure Maps is on, the badge reads "Azure Maps" and shows both sampled times.
The matrix is read, never written. A typed capacity stays on its line and never pushes back to the table.
