A figure you typed stays yours — a new route or a new allowance will not quietly overwrite it. Automatic figures refresh whenever the route, the allowance, capacity, trucks or quantity change. "Return to automatic" is how a typed figure goes back.

The finder ranks on straight-line distance, which is fast and free. Once you select a tip, the line saves that facility and prices it on the real route — so the line reads 21.6 km where the finder said 16.8 km. The screen says exactly that under the list, so nobody reads it as a discrepancy.

What this changes, for you to confirm

The allowance is modelled, and labelled that way. Geoapify gives a busy-road estimate, not measured traffic on the day. The wording on screen says so, everywhere the number appears.
Kilometres never move with the allowance. Section 2 is the proof panel, and it is a test as well as a picture.
Fuel figures will change on existing lines. Today a final part-day is billed as a full day of driving. After this it is billed for the trips that happen. Day hire is untouched.
"Find tip" now actually sticks. Today it resolves a tip and throws the id away, so the line keeps a name and a straight-line distance. After this, selecting a tip behaves exactly like choosing it on the row.
