**What it fixes.** Third slice of `charge-steps-correctness`. The charge-step
editor could be walked into a state it could not leave: removing step 1 deleted
the only `start`, and `addStep` appends, so there was no way to put one back at
the front — the only exit was to delete every remaining step and retype the rule.
Three guards close it: a step reordered into slot 0 is coerced to `start`; step 1
renders no remove control and `removeStep` refuses index 0; and `start` leaves the
add menu once the list is non-empty.

**Two deliberate divergences from the approved mock-up, both because the mock-up
is wrong.** Its coercion leaves the displaced `start` at index 1 — a second
`start`, which is the exact bug guard 3 exists to close, and which saves cleanly
then silently discards everything computed before it. The build exchanges operands
instead. And a step carrying no operand (`round`, `floor`, `cap`) cannot be coerced
to `start` at all; the mock-up would produce an operandless `start` that fails
validation, so those moves are refused and the arrow disabled. **Marco asked for
the ERP to match the mock-ups as closely as possible; these two places do not, and
that is the reason.**
