**The defect.** Slice 5 (#1646) relocated the measurement fields into a block that
starts closed by design, and left `+ Add measurement` as the only per-item opener —
a button that both added and revealed. So an estimator who wanted to **look at**
three existing measurements had to click a button that appended a blank fourth and
PATCHed it to the server. **Reading required writing**, on a control whose tick and
count exist precisely to say "this item already has these". Confirmed before
building rather than assumed: `itemOpenBlocks` starts empty, no effect opens a block
because an item has data, and that button is the only call site of
`openItemBlock(…, "measurement")` in the repo.

**The fix.** Closed reveals and writes nothing; open appends, because that is the
only state where the estimator can see what they are adding to. The first click on
an *unmeasured* item is unchanged. The rule lives in one pure helper,
`revealOrAddPatch`; all three action buttons route through it, and comment and ACM
pass no add-patch so their behaviour is bit-identical.

**The label changes with the state** — closed with a count now reads
**"Show measurements"**. A leading `+` promises mutation, and after this fix that
click does not mutate.

**Evidence.** Four unit cases assert on the **PATCH not being issued**, not merely
on the block opening — that is the whole point of the fix. The guard was
mutation-checked: deleting the closed-returns-null line fails two of them, which
was verified and then restored. The e2e helper had depended on the append (it
waited for the appended row 2 to prove the refetch landed); it now settles on the
block and row 1. No assertion was weakened, and every `toHaveCount(0)`
pre-assertion survives — those are what prove the block starts closed.
