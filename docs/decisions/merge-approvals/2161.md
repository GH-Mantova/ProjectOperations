**What it is.** Scope-cards S8g. Haulage travel time stops being a straight line multiplied by a
road factor. A new Geoapify routing provider resolves each leg twice - free-flow and approximated -
and the ratio of the two becomes a traffic index (floored at 1.00, two decimal places) that an
estimator can see and override on the line. Four pure functions in `travel-time.ts` carry the
arithmetic: planning minutes as the average of the baseline and the baseline times the index,
required trips as quantity over capacity rounded up, duration in days, and total trip km.

**The arithmetic correction that matters commercially.** Fuel was charged on
`dailyKm x durationDays x trucks`, a full-day figure, so a job was billed fuel for trips that never
happened. It is now charged on `totalTripKm` - trips x 2 x one-way km - which is immutable under
the traffic index: a heavier index makes the job take longer, not travel further. Rows written
before this slice, which carry no resolved route, keep the legacy term.

**Failure mode.** The provider is selected by configuration. If the Geoapify key is absent, or its
plan refuses the Routing API, the factory selects the straight-line provider and every affected
line carries the estimated badge - a badge on a line, not a broken save and not a wrong price.
That the key's plan permits routing rests on Marco's statement and was not independently measured
by any station; the fallback is what makes that acceptable.
